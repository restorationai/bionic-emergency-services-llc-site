## Restoration emergencies common in Tomball

Tomball sits in the Gulf Coast humidity belt, which means two things work against a house at the same time: heavy seasonal rainfall and air that rarely dries out enough to help. Slab foundations common throughout the area shift as the clay-heavy soil swells and contracts between wet and dry stretches, which opens hairline cracks that let water track under flooring long before anyone notices a stain. Add in hurricane-season downpours and the occasional frozen-pipe event during a hard winter cold snap, and you get the two calls we see most from this area: water intrusion that started at a slab crack or roof flashing, and pipe failures tied to sudden temperature swings. Humidity also means mold has less time to wait once a leak goes undetected. Materials that stay damp more than a day or two in this climate are already at risk.

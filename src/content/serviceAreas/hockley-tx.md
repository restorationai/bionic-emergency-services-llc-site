## Restoration emergencies common in Hockley

Hockley sits on the western edge of Harris County where new subdivisions are going up fast alongside older rural homesteads, and that mix drives two different kinds of calls. Newer slab homes on expansive clay soil see foundation movement that cracks slab plumbing and shows up as a damp spot that's actually been wicking moisture into drywall and baseboards for weeks. Older properties on the outskirts, some on well water and septic, are more prone to slow plumbing failures and crawlspace moisture that goes unnoticed until it smells. Gulf Coast humidity means any of these leaks can turn into secondary damage like swelling, warping, and lingering odors if they sit, and the spring and early-summer storm pattern that rolls through this part of the county brings wind-driven rain, hail, and the occasional downed tree limb straight into a roof or fence line.

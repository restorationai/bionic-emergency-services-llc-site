## Restoration emergencies common in Bellaire

Bellaire's location on the Texas Gulf Coast means heavy, slow-moving rain events and tropical systems are a regular part of the calendar, and the area's expansive clay soil swells and shrinks with moisture swings in ways that stress slab foundations and underground plumbing. Combine that with a housing stock where a large share of homes were built from the 1940s through the 1960s, and you get recurring patterns: slab leaks that go undetected for weeks, foundation movement that cracks pipe joints, and attic or wall cavities that trap humidity long after a storm passes. Flat or low-slope commercial roofs in the area are also prone to ponding and slow leaks that show up as ceiling stains long before anyone notices a roof problem.

Much of Bellaire's housing was built from the 1940s through the 1960s, with brick veneer and wood-frame construction over slab-on-grade foundations, a standard that largely continues in newer infill homes and renovations.

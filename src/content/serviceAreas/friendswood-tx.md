## Restoration emergencies common in Friendswood

Friendswood sits along Clear Creek in the flat, low-lying stretch of the Gulf Coast south of Houston, which means drainage is slow even in an ordinary rainstorm. Outside of named storms, the region's humid subtropical climate keeps indoor humidity high for much of the year, which shortens the window before a slow leak behind a wall or under a slab turns into secondary damage. Slab foundations built on expansive Gulf Coast clay also shift with wet-dry cycles, which stresses plumbing joints and can turn a small supply line crack into a hidden, ongoing water loss.

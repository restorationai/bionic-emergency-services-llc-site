## Why Humble Properties See Water Damage

Humble sits close to where the San Jacinto River feeds into Lake Houston, and that proximity shapes how water behaves once it gets inside a structure. Groundwater levels run high for much of the year, which means crawlspaces and slab-on-grade foundations common in the area stay damp longer after a leak than they would in a drier inland city. The Houston region's heavy clay soil also shifts with rainfall, which can stress plumbing joints and slab penetrations over time, a slow setup for the sudden pipe failures we get called out for. Add in Gulf Coast humidity that sits above 70 percent most of the year, and any water intrusion we don't extract quickly becomes a drying problem instead of a mopping problem.

## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Humble

We start with extraction, pulling standing water with truck-mounted equipment before it has a chance to wick further into baseboards and subfloor. From there we take moisture readings on framing, drywall, and flooring materials, because in this climate a surface that looks dry can still be holding water in the wall cavity. Our technicians are IICRC-certified in water restoration and structural drying, so we set drying equipment according to the IICRC S500 standard rather than guessing at placement, which matters in homes where humidity outside is already working against the dry-down. We recheck moisture levels daily until the structure reads dry, not just until it looks dry.

## Humble Insurance Coordination

A lot of water losses in this part of Harris County involve a mix of homeowners and flood coverage, and the two don't always pay for the same damage. We photograph and document the loss as we find it, room by room and material by material, so your adjuster has what they need whether you're filing under a standard water damage claim or a separate flood policy through the NFIP. We can't tell you how your specific policy will respond, but we can make sure the documentation doesn't become the reason a claim gets delayed.

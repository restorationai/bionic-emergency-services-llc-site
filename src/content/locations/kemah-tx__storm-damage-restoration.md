**Storm damage emergency in Kemah?** Call now for emergency service. Kemah sits right on Galveston Bay at the mouth of Clear Lake, which means the same wind event that knocks down a few branches inland can push storm surge up through marina neighborhoods and waterfront lots here. Storm damage restoration in a bay town like this isn't just about wind and rain, it's about what happens when brackish water gets into a structure that was never built to dry out fast.

## Why Kemah Properties See Storm Damage Issues

Kemah's exposure to Galveston Bay means homes and businesses take on wind-driven rain, storm surge, and debris impact in ways that inland Houston suburbs rarely do. Many properties near the waterfront are built on pilings or elevated slabs specifically because of surge risk, but that doesn't protect ground-level garages, boat houses, and lower decks from taking water during a tropical system. Add in the area's dense tree canopy along residential streets, and a single severe thunderstorm can bring down limbs onto roofs while also driving rain sideways under flashing that was fine the day before. Salt-laden air off the bay also means metal fasteners, HVAC components, and exposed framing corrode faster once they've been wet, which changes how quickly a job needs to move from drying to repair.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Kemah

We start with a structural assessment to separate what's cosmetic from what's compromised, because a bay-front property with surge exposure often has moisture traveling further into wall cavities and subfloor than it looks like from the surface. Emergency tarping or board-up comes first if the roof or exterior envelope is open, followed by removal of waterlogged debris, insulation, and flooring that can't be salvaged. Drying is handled to the IICRC S500 standard, with extra attention to assemblies near the waterline where brackish intrusion is a factor, since saltwater residue left behind can continue to draw moisture and corrode metal long after the structure reads dry on a meter. Our IICRC-trained technicians document moisture readings throughout so there's a clear record for the adjuster.

## Kemah Insurance & HOA Coordination

Coastal Texas properties frequently carry separate windstorm coverage through the Texas Windstorm Insurance Association alongside a standard homeowner's policy, and that split matters after a storm because wind damage and storm surge or flood damage are often adjusted differently and sometimes by different carriers entirely. We photograph and document damage in a way that supports whichever claim path applies, whether that's a standard wind claim or a flood policy claim running in parallel. For waterfront communities with an HOA, we've found many have specific rules about dumpster placement, tarp colors, and contractor access near shared docks or seawalls, so we check with the property manager or HOA board before staging equipment when a job calls for it.

## Local note

On pier-and-beam or elevated homes near the Kemah waterfront, storm surge debris frequently collects underneath the structure in the crawlspace rather than inside the living space, and homeowners sometimes miss it because the main floor looks untouched. We always check under-structure areas on elevated bay-front properties after a surge event, since trapped debris and standing water there can keep humidity elevated in the floor system for weeks if it's not cleared and dried properly.

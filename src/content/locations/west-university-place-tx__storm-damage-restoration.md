## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in West University Place

We start with a structural assessment, roof and exterior envelope first, because wind driven rain in this region typically gets in through a lifted shingle, a damaged ridge vent, or a cracked chimney flashing before it ever shows up as a ceiling stain. Downed trees or large limbs get addressed for immediate safety, and any active leaks are tarped or boarded so the interior stops absorbing moisture while repairs are scheduled. From there we move to extraction and drying, following IICRC S500 drying principles, with moisture mapping to confirm what's actually wet versus what just looks wet on the surface. Older West University Place homes with plaster ceilings or original wood subfloors hold moisture longer than newer drywall and OSB construction, so we adjust drying timelines and equipment placement accordingly rather than pulling equipment on a fixed schedule.

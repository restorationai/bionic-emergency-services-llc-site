## Why Seabrook Sees Storm Damage Differently Than Inland Houston

Seabrook's exposure is a mix of storm surge, wind, and standing rainwater, and each one damages a house differently. Surge pushes bay water through slab penetrations and under exterior doors, wind peels shingles and soffit off roofs that face the open water with little tree break, and slow-moving systems that stall over Galveston Bay can dump rain faster than storm drains in older residential streets can carry it off. Houses built before modern wind-rated roofing codes took hold along the Gulf Coast are more likely to lose roof covering in a straight-line wind event, which turns a roof problem into a ceiling and insulation problem within a single storm.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Seabrook

The first visit is about stopping the damage from spreading, not just documenting it. That means tarping an open roof section or board-up on a broken window or door before the next rain band moves through, then extracting any standing water with the same water-removal methods used on flood jobs. Once the structure is stable, our technicians, trained to IICRC water restoration and structural drying standards, set drying equipment and track moisture readings in framing and subfloor rather than relying on how a surface looks or feels. Debris from tree limbs and roof material gets cleared from the property line and gutters so a second storm in the same system doesn't create new entry points. Every step gets photographed for the insurance file as we go, not reconstructed afterward.

## Seabrook Insurance & HOA Coordination

Many Seabrook properties carry separate wind/hail and flood policies, and which one applies often depends on whether the damage came from rain entering through a wind-created opening or from surge and groundwater pushing in from outside. We document both the cause and the water line or debris pattern so your adjuster has what's needed to route the claim to the right policy, and we work directly with most major carriers on billing so the claim isn't entirely on you to manage while you're also dealing with a damaged house. For properties inside waterfront HOAs with exterior repair approval requirements, we can provide the photo documentation and scope notes those boards typically ask for before exterior work starts.

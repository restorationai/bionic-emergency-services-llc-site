## Why Tomball Properties See Storm Damage Issues

Tomball's growth has layered newer subdivisions over older, established streets closer to downtown, which means we see two very different storm problems depending on the block. Older homes tend to have roofing and flashing systems that are past their original service life, so wind-driven rain finds its way through nail pops and aging shingles long before the storm is even over. Newer construction on the outskirts often sits on heavier clay soil that doesn't drain quickly, so after a few inches of rain the water has nowhere to go but toward the slab. Add in Tomball's dense tree cover and it's common for a single storm to produce both wind-driven water intrusion and impact damage from falling limbs on the same afternoon.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Tomball

We start with emergency tarping and board-up to stop active water intrusion, because a hole in the roof during a second wave of rain turns a contained problem into a whole-house one. From there our crews move to water extraction and moisture mapping, drying structural materials in line with the IICRC S500 standard rather than guessing based on how a wall or ceiling looks on the surface. Tree debris gets cleared enough to assess the roof deck and framing underneath, and we document every affected area with photos for the insurance file before any rebuild conversations start. Our technicians hold IICRC WRT and ASD credentials, which matters most on storm jobs where water has traveled into wall cavities or insulation that isn't visible from the room.

## Tomball Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most Tomball storm claims we see involve wind and hail damage to roofing alongside water intrusion, and carriers want clear documentation of what happened and when. We photograph and log conditions as we find them so the paper trail supports the claim rather than leaving gaps an adjuster has to question. A number of Tomball's newer planned communities also have HOA guidelines around how quickly tarping, debris removal, or temporary exterior repairs need to happen after a storm, and we coordinate our timeline so homeowners aren't caught between an insurance process and an HOA deadline.

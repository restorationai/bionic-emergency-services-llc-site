## Why Piney Point Village Properties See Storm Damage Issues

Piney Point Village sits among Houston's Memorial Villages, a wooded, low-density area that traces back to large estate lots and a tree canopy that's decades older than most of the surrounding suburbs. That canopy is an asset until a derecho or hurricane outer band rolls through: heavy limbs come down on roofs, gutters clog with leaf litter and pine needles, and ponding water finds its way under flashing that was never designed for sustained saturation. The clay-heavy soil common across this part of Harris County also swells and contracts with heavy rain cycles, which can shift foundations just enough to open new gaps around window and door frames, the kind of entry point that turns a windy afternoon into a slow water intrusion problem over the following days.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Piney Point Village

The first visit is about stopping further loss: tarping exposed roof sections, boarding broken windows, and extracting any standing water before it works into subfloor and insulation. From there we document everything with photos and moisture readings, because on properties this size the adjuster conversation is usually about scope, not whether the damage is real. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with placement of air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to room size and material type rather than a one-size approach, since a formal dining room with plaster detailing dries differently than a garage or utility space. Our crews hold IICRC WRT and ASD credentials, which matters most on jobs where structural drying has to be verified, not guessed at.

## Piney Point Village Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most storm claims in this area involve wind, hail, or fallen-tree damage, and carriers want clear documentation tying the damage to the storm event itself, not pre-existing wear. We photograph and log conditions before any tarping or debris work begins so that record exists before anything changes. Piney Point Village, like the other Memorial Villages, maintains deed restrictions and permitting requirements through the city rather than an HOA in the traditional sense, and large properties often have setback and screening rules that affect where a dumpster or drying equipment can stage during the job. We coordinate with homeowners on placement up front rather than finding out mid-job that a driveway or side yard isn't an option.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Deer Park

We start with a walk-through to separate what's structurally sound from what isn't, then move into content triage, board-up or tarping if the structure is exposed, and soot removal calibrated to the surface involved. Hard, non-porous surfaces get dry-sponge or chemical sponge cleaning first; porous materials, drywall, carpet, and upholstery, usually require a judgment call on restore versus replace, especially once smoke has mixed with the area's ambient humidity and started to set into fibers. Odor control comes next, using thermal fogging or ozone treatment depending on how deep the smoke penetrated, and we dry the structure to the IICRC S500 standard before any rebuild work begins. On Deer Park jobs specifically, we pay close attention to HVAC systems, since a single heating cycle can redistribute soot through every room the ductwork touches.

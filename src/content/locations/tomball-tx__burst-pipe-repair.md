## Why Tomball Properties See Burst Pipe Issues

Most homes north of Houston, including those throughout Tomball, sit on slab foundations poured directly over the region's heavy clay soil. That clay swells when saturated and shrinks during dry spells, and the constant movement puts stress on both the slab and the plumbing embedded in or under it. Add in the occasional hard freeze, the kind that hit the Houston area during Winter Storm Uri in 2021, and older copper or galvanized supply lines that weren't insulated for sustained sub-freezing temperatures become a real liability. Homes built before PEX repiping became standard are especially prone to splitting at fittings and elbows once temperatures drop and then rebound quickly, which is a common pattern across this part of Texas.

## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Tomball

The first step on any burst pipe call is locating and stopping the source, then mapping how far the water has actually traveled. On a slab foundation that often means checking adjacent rooms and closets for moisture migration under flooring, not just the room where the leak surfaced. We extract standing water, pull baseboards and flooring where needed to access saturated material, and set up structural drying equipment calibrated to the humidity and temperature in that part of the house. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with moisture readings logged daily so we know when framing, subfloor, and drywall have actually returned to normal rather than just looking dry on the surface. Our technicians hold IICRC WRT and ASD training, which matters most on jobs where water has gotten into wall cavities or under cabinetry and isn't visible without a meter.

## Tomball Insurance Coordination

Sudden pipe breaks are typically covered under a standard homeowner's policy as accidental discharge of water, which is different from the gradual-leak exclusions that trip people up on slow plumbing failures. We document the break point, the extent of water travel, and the materials affected with photos and moisture readings before any demolition starts, since that record is what most adjusters ask for first. We bill most major carriers directly so homeowners aren't floating the full repair cost while a claim processes.

## Local note

One thing that trips up homeowners in this area: because so many Tomball properties are slab-on-grade, a pipe break under the kitchen can show up as a damp spot in a bedroom on the opposite side of the house, simply because water follows the path of least resistance along utility trenches poured into the slab. We always check beyond the obvious wet spot before calling a job finished, because a slab leak that reads "small" on the surface can mean a much larger subsurface path that needs tracing before repair.

## Why Montgomery Properties See Water Damage Issues

Montgomery County's expansive clay soil swells when it's wet and shrinks when it dries, and that constant movement stresses slab foundations and the plumbing running through them. A lot of the older homes in and around Montgomery were built pier-and-beam rather than slab, which means a supply line failure or water heater leak often drains straight into the crawlspace instead of pooling visibly on a floor. We've found that crawlspace moisture goes undetected longer here than in slab-built neighborhoods, simply because nobody's looking under the house. Add in well water and septic systems common outside the city's water lines, and a failed pressure tank or a septic backup brings its own set of contamination concerns on top of the water itself. Summer humidity off Lake Conroe doesn't help either: once materials get wet, the air itself is already near saturation, which slows natural evaporation and lets materials stay wet longer, raising the risk of secondary damage if drying doesn't happen fast.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Montgomery

We start by identifying whether the loss is isolated to a slab-level event or has traveled into a crawlspace, since that changes how we stage extraction equipment. Standing water gets pulled first with truck-mounted or portable extraction units, and we check flooring, baseboards, and subfloor for the moisture that doesn't show on the surface. For pier-and-beam homes we get into the crawlspace directly, because drying a vented, humid crawlspace in Montgomery's climate takes different airflow and dehumidification than drying an open living room. Our technicians work to the IICRC S500 standard for water damage, measuring moisture daily rather than guessing when a space is dry, and we hold off on reconstruction until readings confirm the structure has actually returned to a normal moisture range, not just a dry-to-the-touch surface.

Montgomery homes on well systems sometimes have a pressure tank in an outbuilding or crawlspace access rather than a utility closet, and when that tank fails it can run for hours before anyone hears it, since there's no city water meter spinning to flag the loss.

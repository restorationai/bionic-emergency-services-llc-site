## Why Humble Properties See Storm Damage

Much of Humble's housing stock was built from the 1970s through the 1990s on slab foundations, a style that handles normal Gulf Coast humidity fine but struggles when wind-driven rain gets up under roof decking or siding during a tropical system. The area's heavy clay soil doesn't drain quickly, so yards and crawlspaces can stay saturated for days after a storm passes, which keeps pulling moisture into framing long after the rain stops. Add in the number of large pine and oak trees planted close to homes in older subdivisions, and wind events regularly mean both a roof breach and a fallen tree to deal with in the same visit.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Humble

We start with a structural safety check, because a tree-damaged roof or a wind-lifted section of decking isn't safe to walk until it's confirmed stable, and we tarp or board openings before water intrusion gets worse.

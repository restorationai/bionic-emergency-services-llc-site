Aldine sits on flat, clay-heavy ground just north of Houston, the kind of soil that holds water against a slab instead of letting it drain, and that changes how a burst pipe or storm backup behaves once it's inside a home. Whether it's a supply line failure under a kitchen sink or standing water from a heavy rain event, the clock on drywall, baseboards, and subfloor starts the moment the water sits still. BIONIC Emergency Services handles the extraction and structural drying, and we work the job the way the soil and the housing stock in this area actually demand.

## Why Aldine Properties See Water Damage Issues

Aldine is unincorporated Harris County, which means drainage infrastructure, permitting, and flood control fall to the county rather than a city public works department. That matters because the area drains toward Greens Bayou, and homes closer to that watershed see slower runoff during heavy rain, which pushes water toward foundations that were often poured on expansive clay. Clay soil swells when wet and shrinks when dry, and over years that movement opens hairline gaps around slab penetrations and exterior walls where water finds a way in. Add Houston's long humid season and a summer thunderstorm pattern that can dump several inches in an hour, and you get two common calls: slow seepage around slab edges that homeowners don't notice until flooring starts to cup, and sudden interior flooding when a line fails or gutters can't keep up with a downpour.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Aldine

The first step on any call is pulling standing water with extraction equipment sized to the loss, not a shop vac approach. From there we check moisture content in the slab, baseboards, and wall cavities, because in this area the slab itself often holds moisture longer than the visible surface suggests, especially in homes built before raised foundation requirements became common. We set drying equipment based on those readings and the IICRC S500 drying standard rather than a fixed number of days, since clay-soil moisture intrusion can re-wet a slab faster than homes on better-draining ground. Air movers and dehumidifiers stay in place until moisture meters confirm the structure is back to a dry standard, not just until it looks and feels dry to the touch.

## Insurance Coordination for Aldine Homeowners

Most water losses here come through either a plumbing failure, covered under a standard homeowners policy, or storm-related intrusion, which can fall into a gray area depending on whether the water came from above or rose from the ground. We document moisture readings, affected materials, and the source of the water at every stage, which gives your adjuster what they need to process the claim without a second site visit. If your policy requires separate flood coverage because of Greens Bayou proximity or county floodplain mapping, we note that distinction clearly in our documentation so there's no confusion about which coverage applies.

## Local note

A lot of homes in the Aldine area were built on pier-and-beam or slab foundations before Harris County updated its drainage and grading standards, and that older construction doesn't always shed water away from the house the way newer builds do. When we get a call from one of these older properties, we check under-house crawlspaces and perimeter grading as part of the initial assessment, because water that looks like it came from inside the home sometimes traces back to grading that pools water against the foundation after every storm.

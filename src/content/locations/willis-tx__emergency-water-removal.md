## Why Willis Properties See Water Removal Issues

Willis sits in Montgomery County just off I-45, close enough to Lake Conroe that a lot of the housing stock here is lake-adjacent: cabins, weekend homes, and year-round residences built with crawlspaces or pier-and-beam foundations rather than the slab construction you'd find in denser Houston suburbs. Those crawlspaces trap moisture after a pipe failure or heavy rain event far longer than a slab would, and the region's humid, subtropical climate means that trapped water lingers and starts causing secondary damage instead of drying out on its own. On the slab side, the expansive clay soils common across this part of Texas shift with wet-dry cycles, which stresses plumbing joints under the slab and is a frequent, quiet cause of slow leaks that go undetected until flooring starts to cup or baseboards start to swell and discolor.

## Our Water Removal Process in Willis

The first call is always triage: what's the water source, is it clean, gray, or contaminated, and how long has it been sitting. From there our crew extracts standing water with truck-mounted or portable extraction equipment, then moves into moisture mapping, checking subfloor, crawlspace framing, and wall cavities with meters rather than guessing from surface appearance. In Willis specifically, that often means getting underneath a pier-and-beam home to check joists and insulation that a slab-built house in a tighter subdivision wouldn't have. Drying follows IICRC S500 guidelines, with air movers and dehumidifiers placed and monitored daily until materials read dry, not just damp to the touch. Homes near the lake sometimes need an extra drying cycle because ambient humidity off the water slows evaporation even after extraction is complete.

## Local note

One thing we watch for in Willis that's less of a concern in tighter urban subdivisions: pier-and-beam and crawlspace homes near Lake Conroe hold ground moisture longer after a leak because the ventilation under the house is often minimal and the surrounding soil stays saturated during wet seasons.

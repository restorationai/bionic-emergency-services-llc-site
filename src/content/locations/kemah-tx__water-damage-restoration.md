## Why Kemah Properties See Water Damage Issues

Kemah's housing stock skews toward older waterfront cottages and newer construction raised on pilings or pier-and-beam foundations to meet flood elevation requirements near the bay. Both building types create their own water problems. Raised homes trap moisture underneath in the crawlspace, where it can sit against joists and subfloor insulation long after a storm passes. Older slab homes closer to Second Street and the harbor area tend to have plumbing and tie-ins that predate current code, which means a slow leak behind a wall can go unnoticed for weeks in the Gulf Coast's high humidity before anyone sees staining or notices the odor. Add in seasonal tropical systems that push bay water inland and storm drains that back up faster than Houston's, and Kemah properties face a water intrusion pattern that's as much about the bay as it is about the roof.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Kemah

The process starts the same way everywhere: stop the source, extract standing water, and get moisture readings on every affected material. What changes in Kemah is the drying phase. Gulf humidity hovering well above 60% for most of the year means ambient air alone will never pull moisture out of drywall or framing fast enough, so we run commercial dehumidification alongside air movers from day one rather than waiting to see if conditions improve. We also check crawlspace and sub-floor moisture separately from interior readings on elevated homes, because a living room can read dry on a moisture meter while the joists below are still saturated. Structural drying here is calibrated to the climate, not just the square footage.

## Kemah Insurance & HOA Coordination

A lot of water losses here involve a flood policy, a homeowners policy, or both, depending on whether the source was rising water or an internal failure like a burst supply line. We document the cause of loss, take moisture maps and photos of affected materials, and provide the kind of documentation carriers ask for when a claim involves both NFIP flood coverage and a standard HO policy. Waterfront HOAs in Kemah often have their own requirements around dock and seawall-adjacent work or dumpster and equipment placement, and we coordinate with property managers on those details before crews arrive for a multi-day drying job.

## Local note

Homes built on pier-and-beam foundations near the water in Kemah are required to sit elevated for flood compliance, which protects the living space in a surge event but creates a long, low crawlspace that holds moisture like a sponge. We've found that interior rooms can register dry on a moisture meter within two or three days while the crawlspace underneath is still fully saturated, so we run a secondary drying setup under the house even after the visible damage upstairs looks resolved. Skipping that step is how framing rot and other hidden damage show up months later in homes that "dried out fine."

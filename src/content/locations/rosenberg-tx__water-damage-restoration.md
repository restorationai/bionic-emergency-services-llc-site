Fort Bend County's expansive clay soil shrinks in the dry months and swells after heavy rain, and that movement stresses slab foundations and the plumbing running through them. A lot of Rosenberg housing stock, from the older sections near downtown to the newer subdivisions further out, sits on slab-on-grade construction, which means a supply line failure often shows up as moisture wicking up through flooring rather than an obvious puddle. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Rosenberg

## Why Cloverleaf Properties See Storm Damage Issues

Cloverleaf sits in the flat, low-lying stretch of Harris County east of downtown Houston, where drainage ditches and bar ditches do a lot of the work that storm sewers handle in other parts of the metro. When a tropical system or a fast-moving thunderstorm line dumps several inches of rain in an hour, those ditches can back up faster than they drain, and water finds its way under slab foundations and through weep holes before anyone notices standing water in the yard. Add in the wind gusts that come with Gulf-fed storms and tornado spin-up events common to this part of Texas, and you get a pattern we see often: roof damage first, then water intrusion that keeps spreading for days after the skies clear. Mature trees throughout the area, planted decades ago on smaller residential lots, also mean storm cleanup frequently involves downed limbs on roofs, fences, and carports, not just wind-driven rain.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Cloverleaf

We start with a structural assessment: roof, exterior walls, windows, and any point where wind-driven rain could have entered. If the home has a pier-and-beam or slab foundation common to this area's mid-century housing stock, we check the crawlspace or slab perimeter for water intrusion before we ever touch the interior. From there it's extraction of any standing water, moisture mapping with meters to find out how far saturation has traveled into drywall, insulation, and subfloor, and structural drying set up according to the IICRC S500 standard. Tree and debris removal from the structure itself (not full yard cleanup) happens early, since a tarp or temporary roof covering only works if the damaged area is clear first. We document every step with photos for your insurance file as we go, not after the fact.

## Cloverleaf Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most storm claims in this part of Harris County move through wind and hail coverage or a separate flood policy, depending on whether the damage came from the roof down or the ground up, and carriers often want that distinction clearly documented. We photograph and log damage by cause, wind versus water, so your adjuster isn't left guessing which policy applies. If your street has an HOA with exterior repair standards (common in some of the newer infill developments mixed into older Cloverleaf blocks), we can coordinate temporary repairs like tarping or board-up so the property stays compliant while the claim is processed.

## Local note

One thing we watch for in this part of Harris County: homes built before modern drainage codes often have yard grading that slopes toward the foundation instead of away from it, a holdover from how these lots were originally platted. After a heavy storm, that grading can trap water against the slab for days even after the rain stops, which is why we check perimeter drainage as part of the initial walk-through, not as an afterthought once drywall is already wet.

## Why South Houston Properties See Water Damage Issues

Much of Harris County, including South Houston, sits on heavy clay soil that locals sometimes call gumbo clay. It expands when saturated and contracts when it dries, which stresses slab foundations and the plumbing running through them. A slow slab leak can go unnoticed for weeks because the moisture spreads laterally under the concrete before it ever shows up as a damp spot on the carpet. Add in a humid subtropical climate with a long hurricane season running roughly June through November, and you get two separate water problems: sudden, heavy rain events that overwhelm flat, slow-draining streets, and chronic humidity that keeps materials from fully drying on their own even after the water is gone. Older homes in this part of Harris County, many built mid-century for workers at the nearby industrial and ship channel operations, often have pier-and-beam or early slab construction with plumbing that has never been updated. That combination of aging pipe, reactive soil, and sustained humidity is why we see as much slow, hidden water damage here as we do sudden flooding.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in South Houston

We start by finding where the water is actually sitting, not just where it's visible, which matters more on slab foundations than almost anywhere else. Moisture meters and thermal imaging let us check under flooring and behind baseboards without tearing out materials that might still be salvageable. Extraction comes next, pulling standing water out of carpet, pad, and any low spots before it has time to wick further into drywall or subflooring. Because of the regional humidity, we set up drying equipment calibrated for a Gulf Coast environment, not a dry-climate default: higher-capacity dehumidifiers and air movers positioned to counter the ambient moisture already in the air, not just the moisture in the structure. Our technicians are IICRC-trained in water restoration and structural drying, and we document moisture readings throughout the job so you have a clear record of when materials actually reached a dry standard, not just when they looked dry.

Most homeowners' and renters' policies in this area cover sudden water damage, like a burst supply line or a failed water heater, but gradual slab leaks are sometimes treated differently depending on the carrier and the age of the plumbing.
